The gap between activity and value

CRM can tell you who to contact. BI can tell you what happened.

The harder decision is whether the action changed the outcome enough to justify its cost.

01

Organic return

Some customers would return without an intervention. Messaging them can be avoidable cost.

02

Selective action

Others may need a reminder, targeted intervention or incentive—with contact and margin guardrails.

03

Incremental evidence

A holdout and explicit economics show what can be described, tested and legitimately claimed.
