# DigiPay proposal V2 — CRM Policy Optimization

## Executive summary

DigiPay’s decision is not “how do we send more messages?” It is “which eligible customers require action, what action is economically justified, and who should receive no action?” MarginLift compares DigiPay’s Current CRM Policy with a selective MarginLift Policy under controlled measurement.

The recommended first sale is a Paid Diagnostic. A Controlled Pilot follows only if data, ownership, finance and experiment gates pass.

## Scope and approach

The initial pilot is one product and one cohort. The design uses a frozen Metric Contract, a treatment/control holdout, ITT analysis, shadow checks, contact caps, exclusions and an approved economic formula. DigiPay CRM remains the execution system; MarginLift is the decision and evidence layer.

## Diagnostic

MarginLift validates data readiness, maps the current policy, identifies organic-return and intervention candidates, checks contact/exclusion risks, evaluates financial completeness, drafts the Metric Contract and delivers a diagnostic Buyer Evidence Package with GO, GO WITH CAVEATS or NO-GO.

## Required data

Diagnostic: hashed customer ID, product/cohort signal, historical action, outcome/value fields and available cost fields. Pilot: assignment, exposure timestamp, delivery status/timestamp, outcome timestamp/status, contact-cap/exclusion fields and lineage snapshot. Optional enrichment includes risk restrictions and historical experiment assignment.

## Economics and evidence

Incremental economics are calculated only from a DigiPay-approved formula and complete FinancialProvenance. Historical analysis is descriptive/shadow; it is not causal proof. The final package records the Metric Contract, integrity assessment, observed results, authorized claims, limitations and audit lineage.

## Responsibilities

MarginLift owns decision architecture, measurement, assignment design, integrity and evidence. DigiPay CRM/Growth owns policy definition, campaign execution and exposure logging. DigiPay Data owns extraction/validation and outcomes. DigiPay Finance approves the formula and costs. Risk/Security reviews processing topology where needed.

## Security boundary

Hashed IDs and batch-first processing are supported. Buyer-controlled or VPC processing can be evaluated during security/deployment review; it is outside the default Diagnostic scope and is not a turnkey commitment.

## Timeline and acceptance

Scoping, data intake, validation and Diagnostic timing depend on buyer availability. A Pilot adds contract freeze, pre-launch checks, execution, outcome window and readout. Diagnostic acceptance is delivery of the agreed readiness package and recommendation, not positive uplift. Pilot acceptance is a valid integrity assessment, financial evaluation and Evidence Package, regardless of result direction.

## Commercial structure and next step

Recommended structure: fixed-fee Diagnostic, separately priced Pilot, optional negotiated Diagnostic credit. Final quote follows confirmation of product, cohort, sources, outcome window, owners and security path. Next step: a working session with CRM/Growth, Data, Finance, business/product and Risk/Security stakeholders.

## Exclusions

CRM replacement, campaign execution, guaranteed uplift/profit, turnkey VPC deployment, enterprise multi-tenancy and unrelated use cases are excluded unless separately scoped.
