# DigiPay Controlled Pilot Offer

**Entry:** a completed Diagnostic with a valid frozen Metric Contract, control/holdout, finance formula, owners and pre-launch integrity approval.

**Scope:** one product, one cohort, Current CRM Policy versus MarginLift Policy, shadow/pre-launch validation, live holdout, ITT outcome analysis and final Buyer Evidence Package.

**Responsibilities:** DigiPay CRM executes; DigiPay Data supplies assignments/exposure/outcomes; DigiPay Finance reconciles economics; MarginLift supplies decision policy, integrity assessment and evidence.

**Acceptance:** frozen contract, assignment and integrity records, outcome analysis, FinancialProvenance evaluation, limitations and authorized decision. A negative or inconclusive result is still valid pilot delivery.

**Commercial model:** separately priced fixed-fee pilot with milestones at kickoff/contract freeze, launch readiness and final package. Diagnostic credit may be negotiated.

**Stop/exit gates:** control contamination, risk-guardrail failure, unresolved finance or invalid integrity produce a safe stop/re-run decision, not an unsupported policy claim.
