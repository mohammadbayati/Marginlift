# DigiPay Paid Diagnostic Offer

**Purpose:** determine whether one DigiPay product/cohort can support a profitable controlled policy comparison.

**Inputs:** safe hashed-ID sample/schema, current CRM policy, target outcome, cost/margin inputs, contact/exclusion rules and named CRM, Data, Finance, Outcome and (if needed) Risk/Security owners.

**MarginLift delivers:** Data Readiness Assessment, Current Policy Map, financial readiness, experiment feasibility, draft Metric Contract, risk/gap register, diagnostic Buyer Evidence Package and GO / GO WITH CAVEATS / NO-GO recommendation.

**Timeline:** recommended working-time range is agreed after data review; buyer waiting time is separate and depends on access and review.

**Commercial model:** recommended fixed fee; 50% kickoff / 50% accepted delivery. Final quote is buyer-dependent and not buyer-approved by default.

**Acceptance:** all listed deliverables are complete, gaps are explicit and the recommendation is issued. No uplift guarantee.

**Out of scope:** live campaign execution, CRM replacement, turnkey VPC, unrelated cohorts/products and unapproved PII onboarding.

**Next step:** confirm one product, one cohort, owners, data sample and finance formula.
