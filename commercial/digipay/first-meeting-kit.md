# INTERNAL SALES PREPARATION — NOT CUSTOMER EVIDENCE

## Opening

“The question is not whether DigiPay can send more CRM messages. It is which eligible customers need which action, and where action is economically justified versus the Current CRM Policy.”

Confirm: one product; one cohort; KPI/window; CRM execution owner; Data/Finance/Outcome owners; formula and cost sources; holdout feasibility; contact/risk restrictions; security path.

Likely objections: CRM already does this; show uplift first; VPC; why pay for Diagnostic; who executes. Use the common negotiation playbook.

Desired first commitment: a 60-minute scoping workshop and safe hashed-ID sample review.

Walk away / pause: no measurable decision, no control path for intended causal Pilot, no Finance participation, or no safe data path.
