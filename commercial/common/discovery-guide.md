# 30-minute discovery call (internal)

0–5 minutes — business context; 5–10 — current CRM policy; 10–15 — target outcome; 15–20 — data/experiment feasibility; 20–25 — economics/Finance; 25–30 — next step.

Critical questions:

1. What decision do you make today, and what would change if it improved?
2. Who receives an action, which action, and who receives no action?
3. What would count as the primary outcome and when would it be observed?
4. Which customers might convert without intervention?
5. What are the current control/no-action rules?
6. Can a holdout be preserved without operational workarounds?
7. Which assignment, exposure, delivery and outcome fields exist?
8. What costs belong in the financial formula?
9. Who approves margin and cost assumptions?
10. Which CRM/Growth, Data, Finance and Outcome owners participate?
11. What volume and history are available? (Buyer confirmation required.)
12. Are contact caps, consent or risk restrictions relevant?
13. Is batch transfer acceptable for the Diagnostic?
14. What security/deployment review is required?
15. What is the next concrete commitment: sample, workshop or scope review?

Internal only. Do not promise uplift, ROI, VPC deployment or CRM execution.
