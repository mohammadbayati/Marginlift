# Diagnostic Evidence Package handoff

Required sections: Executive Decision; Data Readiness; Current Policy; Experiment Feasibility; Financial Readiness; Draft Metric Contract; Key Risks; Claims Allowed; Claims Not Allowed; Recommendation; Next Step; Audit Lineage.

Readout agenda (45–60 minutes): executive decision, findings, data readiness, policy findings, feasibility, economics, risks, Metric Contract proposal, Pilot recommendation and commercial next step.

Buyer questions are answered directly: where is uplift, why holdout, why not scale, why Finance, who executes CRM, and why Pilot is separately priced. Historical findings remain descriptive unless canonical evidence permits stronger claims.
