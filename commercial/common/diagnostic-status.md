# Diagnostic status and stop conditions

Statuses: `NOT_STARTED`, `WAITING_FOR_BUYER`, `DATA_INTAKE`, `DATA_BLOCKED`, `ANALYSIS_IN_PROGRESS`, `FINANCE_INPUT_REQUIRED`, `METRIC_CONTRACT_REVIEW`, `EVIDENCE_PACKAGE_READY`, `BUYER_REVIEW`, `COMPLETE`, `NO_GO`.

Stop/NO-GO conditions: unusable identity linkage; outcome cannot be defined; insufficient history; Finance cannot define economics; no meaningful intervention decision; holdout impossible for a causal Pilot; security/transfer impossible under scope; required ownership unavailable.

A NO-GO Diagnostic is valid paid work when the agreed assessment and Evidence Package are delivered.
