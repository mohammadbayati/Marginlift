# MarginLift commercial model

This is a recommended commercial structure, not market-validated pricing or buyer-approved terms.

## Recommended offer

1. Fixed-fee Paid Diagnostic.
2. Separately priced Controlled Pilot if the diagnostic supports it.
3. Optional negotiated diagnostic credit toward the pilot.

This keeps the first purchase easy to scope, pays for valid measurement even when the answer is “do not proceed,” and avoids making MarginLift’s compensation depend only on positive uplift.

## Quote construction

Use a base scope for one use case, one cohort, one primary KPI, one data snapshot, and one diagnostic Evidence Package. Quote the final amount only after confirming data complexity, owners, security path, and outcome window.

Price drivers are expert time, source-system count, cohort/product count, financial-model complexity, experiment design, security review, pilot duration, and evidence-package scope.

Scope adjusters are additive only when approved: extra cohort/product, extra source, custom integration, extra KPI, custom financial model, security/deployment work, or an extended outcome window.

## Scenario ranges

Use LOW / TARGET / HIGH scenarios in a quote, with the unit and currency agreed with the buyer. Do not present a number as accepted market price. A final quote requires buyer inputs and procurement review.

## Acceptance and payment

Diagnostic: 50% at kickoff and 50% when the agreed readiness package is delivered. Pilot: milestone payments at kickoff/contract freeze, launch readiness, and final Evidence Package. Negative or inconclusive results remain successful delivery when the agreed measurement work is complete.
