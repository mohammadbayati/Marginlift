# Buyer input questionnaire

## Business

Decision, use case, product/cohort, primary KPI, organic outcome possibility, desired decision.

## Data

Hashed ID, event/action/assignment fields, exposure and delivery timestamps, outcomes, value/cost fields, history, snapshot and transfer method.

## CRM

Current policy, treatment actions, no-action behavior, channels, contact caps, consent/exclusions and execution owner.

## Finance

Margin formula, revenue source, incentive/discount, messaging, channel and operating costs, formula approver and currency.

## Experiment

Control/holdout feasibility, assignment unit, randomization, MDE, minimum sample, outcome window, contamination and stopping policy.

## Security

PII boundary, hashed/batch acceptance, retention requirements already defined by policy, processing location and deployment review path.

## Ownership / timeline

CRM/Growth, Data, Finance, Outcome and Security owners; buyer review windows; desired kickoff and procurement constraints.

Blank or unknown answers remain unresolved; they do not become defaults.
