# MilliGold Paid Diagnostic Offer

**Purpose:** determine whether first-purchase data can support a profitable second-purchase controlled pilot.

**Inputs:** hashed customer/event sample, purchase timestamps and sequence, transaction value, current CRM action history, proposed outcome definition/window, cost inputs and Marketing/CRM, Data and Finance owners.

**Deliverables:** purchase-sequence validation, second-purchase/window definition review, data and financial readiness, experiment feasibility, draft Metric Contract, risk/gap register, diagnostic Buyer Evidence Package and GO / GO WITH CAVEATS / NO-GO recommendation.

**Commercial model:** recommended fixed fee, paid regardless of whether a pilot is justified. Payment recommendation: 50% kickoff / 50% accepted delivery. Final quote requires buyer inputs and procurement review.

**Out of scope:** repeat-purchase continuity, live CRM execution, guaranteed outcome, turnkey VPC and unrelated cohorts.

**Next step:** confirm the first-purchase event, second-purchase event, buyer-specific outcome window and anonymous sample.
