# MilliGold proposal V2 — First Purchase to Second Purchase

## Executive summary

The first productized question is narrow: which first-time purchasers require intervention to create an economically justified second purchase? Repeat-purchase continuity is a separate future pathway and is not included in the initial pilot.

## Approach

MarginLift validates an anonymous sample, defines first and second purchase, confirms the buyer-specific outcome window, compares the current CRM policy with a treatment policy, and uses a control/holdout. Historical conversion alone remains descriptive; causal claims require valid experiment evidence.

## Economics

Success is incremental contribution margin versus the current policy, using a MilliGold Finance-approved formula and explicit discount, message, channel and operational costs. Unknown costs are not treated as zero.

## Data and execution

Hashed customer/event IDs, purchase timestamps and sequence, transaction value, assignment/exposure/delivery and second-purchase outcome are sufficient starting points. Batch-first delivery is supported; heavy API/database integration is not required on day one. MilliGold Marketing/CRM executes interventions and records delivery/outcomes.

## Diagnostic and pilot

The Paid Diagnostic validates purchase history, action history, finance inputs, cohort feasibility and the draft Metric Contract. A Controlled Pilot requires one cohort, one pathway, one KPI, a buyer-confirmed outcome window, frozen contract, holdout, integrity monitoring and a final Buyer Evidence Package.

## Responsibilities and acceptance

MarginLift owns measurement, holdout design, integrity and evidence. MilliGold Marketing/CRM owns execution; Data owns event, assignment and outcome data; Finance owns contribution-margin approval. Diagnostic acceptance is complete feasibility evidence, not positive conversion. Pilot acceptance is valid measurement and an authorized decision, regardless of result direction.

## Timing and commercial structure

The outcome window is proposed during scoping and confirmed by MilliGold; no 14-day or 60-day default is assumed. Recommended structure: fixed-fee Diagnostic followed by a separately priced Pilot, with optional negotiated Diagnostic credit. Timeline depends on buyer data and review availability.

## Exclusions and next step

Repeat-purchase continuity, CRM replacement, CRM execution by MarginLift, guaranteed profit, turnkey VPC and unrelated pathways are excluded. Next step: a working session with Marketing/CRM, Data and Finance to select one real campaign, define second purchase and assess an anonymous sample.
