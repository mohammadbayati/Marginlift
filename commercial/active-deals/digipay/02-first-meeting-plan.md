# DigiPay first meeting (30 minutes)

**Objective:** agree the use case and schedule a 60-minute scoping workshop.

| Time | Question | Why | Good signal / warning | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | What CRM decision is costly or uncertain today? | Confirms decision worth measuring | Specific decision / generic growth goal | Narrow to one product |
| 5–10 | Who receives action, no action or incentive today? | Maps current policy | Rules and exclusions documented / intuition only | Request policy map |
| 10–15 | What outcome and window would change a decision? | Defines KPI | Buyer-owned definition / “show uplift” only | Finance + outcome review |
| 15–18 | How could organic return occur without contact? | Protects incrementality | Holdout feasible / no-action impossible | Discuss control |
| 18–21 | Can a one-product, one-cohort holdout be preserved? | Causal feasibility | Yes with contact caps / concurrent campaigns uncontrolled | Record constraint |
| 21–24 | What revenue, margin and action costs are auditable? | Financial provenance | Finance owner and formula / unknown costs | Send finance checklist |
| 24–27 | Who owns CRM execution, data, Finance and outcome? | Prevents handoff failure | Named roles / single unconfirmed owner | Nominate participants |
| 27–29 | What data path and security review are acceptable? | Safe intake | Hashed batch path / PII or VPC demand now | Route to security |
| 29–30 | Will you attend scoping and share schema-level information? | Concrete commitment | Date/owners proposed / indefinite interest | Send follow-up |

Do not demo a full Pilot or imply guaranteed uplift. Reference `commercial/digipay/first-meeting-kit.md` and `commercial/common/discovery-guide.md`.
