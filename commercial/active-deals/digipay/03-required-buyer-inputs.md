# DigiPay required inputs

## Discovery

Use case, product, current CRM policy, target decision/KPI, likely outcome window, CRM/Data/Finance participants, and whether a no-action holdout is feasible.

## Scoping

One-cohort eligibility/exclusions, treatment actions, contact caps/concurrent-campaign policy, historical period, outcome timestamps, cost components, financial formula, security path and timeline assumptions.

## Diagnostic kickoff

Safe hashed-ID schema/sample, data snapshot/version, transfer path, named owners, approved scope/currency/payment decision, and draft Metric Contract inputs.

## Pilot-only

Frozen contract, randomized assignment, live delivery/exposure logs, outcome completeness, Finance-approved provenance, launch approval and separately agreed Pilot terms. Do not request production data before scope and secure transfer are agreed.
