# MilliGold required inputs

## Discovery

First-purchase and second-purchase business question, current CRM action/no-action, likely outcome concept, owners and holdout feasibility.

## Scoping

Canonical event definitions, one-cohort eligibility/exclusions, buyer-confirmed outcome window, treatment and contact rules, purchase/action/delivery history, contribution-margin formula, discount/message costs, security path and timeline.

## Diagnostic kickoff

Safe hashed-ID CSV schema/sample, event timestamps/sequence, snapshot/version, named Marketing/CRM, Data, Finance and outcome owners, agreed scope/currency/payment decision, and Metric Contract inputs.

## Pilot-only

Frozen event/window definitions, randomized holdout, live exposure/delivery logs, complete outcome capture, Finance-approved provenance and separate Pilot terms. Repeat continuity remains future expansion.
