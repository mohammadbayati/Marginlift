# MilliGold procurement and security fast path

## Answer now

Hashed identifiers and batch-first CSV intake are supported; Marketing/CRM retains execution and delivery logging. Initial Diagnostic does not require raw PII where the use case permits it. Evidence remains lineage-aware and fail-closed.

## Requires review

Event-data transfer, retention, deployment topology, legal terms, access controls and any VPC requirement. Outcome-window and contribution-margin definitions require buyer confirmation and Finance approval.

## Not currently promised

Turnkey VPC, enterprise multi-tenancy, certifications, SLA, automatic real-time CRM execution or guaranteed second purchase/profit.

## Procurement checklist

Confirm Diagnostic deliverables, duration assumptions, payment milestones, acceptance criteria, exclusions, owners, change control and the separate Pilot gate. Acceptance is based on valid measurement work, not positive economics.
