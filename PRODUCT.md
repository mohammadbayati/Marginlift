# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MarginLift primarily serves Persian-speaking growth, CRM, retention, and lifecycle leaders at consumer businesses that spend meaningful money on discounts and incentives. Their economic buyers are CMOs and CFOs; their operational partners are analysts and campaign managers.

They usually arrive with campaign exports or customer-level CSV data, limited confidence in causal impact, and pressure to protect revenue while reducing incentive cost. They need a defensible next action, not another reporting dashboard.

## Product Purpose

MarginLift is the decision layer between customer data and campaign execution. It decides where an incentive is likely to change behavior, where a cheaper action is sufficient, and where no action is the most profitable policy.

The product follows one commercial loop: diagnose data readiness and potential waste, decide the next incentive policy, then validate the result with a controlled pilot and outcome readout. Before a valid holdout, financial values are estimates. After a completed pilot, verified incremental impact becomes the basis for scale or stop.

MarginLift does not replace CRM, CDP, campaign execution, or BI. It tells those systems which policy to execute and records the evidence behind that decision.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable.

The product should make a financial decision feel understandable and controlled. The voice is concise, direct, and conservative with claims. Confidence comes from transparent evidence, not technical spectacle.

## Anti-references

- A generic AI marketing dashboard made from oversized KPI cards, purple gradients, glass surfaces, and decorative charts.
- A Dribbble-style e-commerce admin that prioritizes visual density over a clear decision workflow.
- A scientific notebook that leads with Qini, CATE, model names, or statistical jargon before business impact.
- An Apple clone that borrows visual motifs without product purpose.
- A Persian interface with broken half-spaces, Latin digits in executive metrics, mirrored LTR identifiers, or inconsistent RTL alignment.
- A marketing hero embedded inside the authenticated product where it delays routine work.

## Design Principles

1. **Decision first.** Every primary screen answers what changed, why it matters financially, and what to do next.
2. **Proof before claim.** Every financial number carries an evidence level; the interface never presents an observational estimate as causal impact.
3. **Executive clarity, analytical depth.** CMO and CFO outcomes appear first. Model diagnostics remain one level deeper and are always available.
4. **One guided loop.** Data readiness, decisioning, pilot execution, outcome import, and scale/stop read as one continuous workflow.
5. **Restraint earns trust.** Color, motion, cards, and copy are used only when they clarify state, hierarchy, or risk.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product targets WCAG 2.2 AA. All core workflows must work with keyboard navigation, visible focus, 200% text zoom, reduced motion, and high-contrast settings. Persian is the primary language with full RTL layout, correct half-spaces, Persian display digits, and explicit LTR isolation for email addresses, CSV fields, IDs, formulas, and API terms. Mobile and desktop layouts must remain readable without overlap or horizontal page scrolling.
